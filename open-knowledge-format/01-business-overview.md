# Business Overview

## Company

SaffHire Background Screening

## Website

https://www.saffhire.com

## Core positioning

SaffHire provides fast, secure, and reliable background screening services for employers across all industries. The site positions SaffHire as a nationwide background screening provider based in Frisco, Texas.

## Primary offer

Pre-employment and workforce background screening services for businesses, including criminal background checks, verification services, drug screening, MVR/driving record checks, healthcare sanctions/OIG screening, volunteer screening, and related compliance-focused screening content.

## Geographic scope

- Based in Frisco, Texas
- Serves businesses nationwide across all 50 states
- Local SEO pages exist for Frisco, Dallas, Plano, McKinney, and Allen, Texas

## Main website goal

Drive employers and organizations to request a quote, contact SaffHire, or start account setup.

## Important business posture

SaffHire should be presented as helpful, practical, trustworthy, and compliance-aware. The site should not be written as if SaffHire is a law firm or legal advisor. Compliance copy should be careful and should not overpromise outcomes.
