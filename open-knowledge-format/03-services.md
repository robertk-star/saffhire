# Services

This list reflects current public service pages and footer links in the uploaded codebase.

## Core services

### Criminal Background Checks

FCRA-compliant county, state, and federal criminal record searches for employers. Related routes:

- `/criminal-background-checks`
- `/county-criminal-background-checks`
- `/national-criminal-database-search`

### County Criminal Background Checks

County-level criminal court record searches. Important for employers that need more direct courthouse-level criminal record coverage.

### National Criminal Database Search

National database search used as part of a broader screening package. Messaging should avoid implying that a national database search replaces all county or court-level searches.

### Employment Verification

Verification of prior employment details such as dates, job titles, and employment history.

Route: `/employment-verification`

### Education Verification

Verification of degrees, diplomas, school attendance, or credentials.

Route: `/education-verification`

### Drug Screening

DOT and non-DOT drug screening options. Avoid promising a specific panel unless the page or quote specifies it.

Route: `/drug-screening`

### MVR / Driving Records

Motor Vehicle Record checks for roles requiring driving or license review.

Route: `/mvr-checks`

### Healthcare Sanctions / OIG Checks

Healthcare-related screening including sanctions/OIG-focused checks.

Route: `/healthcare-sanctions-oig-checks`

### Volunteer Background Checks

Screening for volunteer organizations, churches, nonprofits, youth-serving groups, and community programs.

Route: `/volunteer-background-checks`

### Small Business Background Checks

Background screening positioned for smaller employers that need practical, affordable screening.

Route: `/small-business-background-checks`

### FCRA Compliance News

Content and updates related to FCRA and screening compliance topics.

Route: `/fcra-news`

## Service messaging rules

- Make clear that employers remain responsible for hiring decisions.
- Do not claim every search is instant.
- Do not state that a national search alone is complete if county or source-level confirmation may be needed.
- For regulated industries, describe screening support rather than making legal guarantees.
