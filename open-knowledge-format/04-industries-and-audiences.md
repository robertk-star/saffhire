# Industries and Audiences

SaffHire should not be positioned as limited to only a few industries. The site should make clear that SaffHire can help companies across many industries.

## Key audience types

### Employers and HR teams

Need fast, accurate, and practical screening workflows.

### Staffing companies

Need repeatable, scalable background screening for many candidates.

### Transportation and trucking companies

Need driver-related screening, MVR checks, DOT/non-DOT drug screening, and safety-focused workflows.

### Healthcare employers

Need screening aligned with healthcare risk, OIG/sanctions needs, license or credential verification, and patient safety concerns.

### Churches, nonprofits, and volunteer organizations

Need volunteer and staff screening for trust, safety, and risk reduction.

### Small businesses

Need simple, affordable background checks without being overwhelmed by enterprise-style systems.

## Audience positioning rule

It is okay to create industry-specific pages and examples, but avoid language that makes SaffHire sound only for those groups. Reinforce that SaffHire helps businesses across all industries.
