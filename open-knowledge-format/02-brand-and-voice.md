# Brand and Voice

## Brand name

Use: SaffHire Background Screening

Acceptable shorter form: SaffHire

## Tone

- Clear
- Professional
- Practical
- Trustworthy
- Helpful
- Business-focused
- Compliance-aware without sounding like legal advice

## Reading level

Use plain language. Prefer direct, easy-to-understand wording over dense compliance jargon.

## Preferred messaging themes

- Hire with confidence
- Fast, secure background screening
- FCRA-aware / FCRA-compliant process language
- Nationwide coverage
- Built for businesses across all industries
- No setup fees and no minimums where applicable
- Results can be fast, but avoid guaranteeing exact turnaround in all cases

## Visual style from current site

- Dark navy sections
- Green call-to-action buttons
- Blue and green brand colors
- Clean professional layout
- Strong headline hierarchy
- Service cards and industry cards
- SaffHire logo from CloudFront currently used in header/footer

## Words and phrases to use carefully

Use caution with:

- "guaranteed"
- "instant results" unless describing a specific instant-capable search
- "fully compliant" if it sounds like a legal guarantee
- "approved" or "cleared" in ways that imply employment decisions are made by SaffHire

## Do not position SaffHire as

- A law firm
- Legal counsel
- A government agency
- A guarantee that an applicant is safe or risk-free
- A company that makes final hiring decisions for employers

## Preferred CTA language

- Get Quote
- Get a Free Quote
- Contact Us
- Create Account
- Start Account Setup
- Learn More
- Talk to SaffHire
