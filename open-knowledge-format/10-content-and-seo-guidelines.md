# Content and SEO Guidelines

## SEO direction

The site has service pages, industry pages, local pages, blogs, guides, sitemap, RSS, robots, structured data, and LLMs text route. Content should support topical authority around employment background screening.

## Blog strategy

Blog topics should help employers understand hiring risk, screening strategy, verification, industry-specific screening, and compliance-aware background screening practices. Avoid writing only FCRA compliance content unless specifically requested.

## Internal linking guidance

When adding content, link to relevant service, industry, guide, and contact pages. Keep CTAs direct and business-focused.

## Page copy guideline

Use the SaffHire brand as a practical helper for employers, not as a fear-based or legal-advice provider. Use examples, but keep wording broad enough to show SaffHire can serve all industries.
