# Compliance and Claim Rules

## High-level compliance posture

SaffHire content should be FCRA-aware and EEOC-aware. The site can say the company supports FCRA-compliant screening workflows, but should not make legal guarantees or offer legal advice.

## Safe language

Use:

- FCRA-compliant background screening services
- Compliance-aware screening workflows
- Helps employers make informed hiring decisions
- Supports safer hiring decisions
- Screening services for employers nationwide
- Employers should follow applicable federal, state, and local requirements

## Avoid or use only with review

Avoid:

- Guaranteed compliance
- We eliminate hiring risk
- We guarantee safe hires
- This search finds every record
- We decide if someone should be hired
- You can reject an applicant based only on this result

## Adverse action / FCRA caution

If content discusses adverse action, pre-adverse action, consumer reports, applicant rights, disputes, or FCRA notices, add careful disclaimers that the information is general and not legal advice.

## Criminal record caution

When writing about criminal records:

- Avoid implying a hit automatically disqualifies a candidate.
- Avoid implying national database checks are complete by themselves.
- Mention that employers should follow applicable laws and internal policies.

## AI/content generation rule

AI-generated blog or social copy should be reviewed before publishing, especially when it discusses compliance, FCRA, EEOC, criminal records, drug testing, healthcare sanctions, or DOT-related topics.
